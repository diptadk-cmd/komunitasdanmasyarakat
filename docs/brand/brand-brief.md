## Arah visual

Dua figur abstrak bermahkota sederhana mengapit monumen/tunas di tengah. Bentuk melambangkan budaya Betawi, kebersamaan komunitas, dan pertumbuhan. Tanpa teks agar logo tetap mudah dipasang berdampingan dengan nama JakSanggar dalam sidebar dan header.

Palet acuan: navy `#123047`, jade `#147D73`, gold `#D5A445`. Ini adalah konsep visual raster, bukan lambang instansi pemerintah. Untuk ikon fungsional antarmuka, gunakan satu set ikon vektor yang konsisten.

## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: standalone square logo symbol for the JakSanggar community and arts dashboard, used in a sidebar at 40 px.
Primary request: create one professional modern minimal geometric emblem inspired by Betawi ondel-ondel and community togetherness. Two abstract human forms gently embrace a single central upright monument or growing shoot. Suggest ondel-ondel only through an extremely simple three-point crown silhouette, never literal detailed faces or costume. Strong unified silhouette.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with alpha, no colored tile.
Style/medium: precision flat vector-like logo, clean geometric curves, solid shapes, crisp edge quality, elegant and practical.
Composition: one single centered compact mark fills about 80% of a square canvas, balanced and approximately symmetrical, generous clear separation between shapes so it stays readable at 40 px. No border.
Color palette: deep navy #123047, jade #147D73, small gold accent #D5A445. Maximum three flat colors.
Text: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, wordmarks.
Constraints: make a useful app logo asset, no poster or presentation sheet, no sample grids, no photographic elements, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no fine lines, no complex details, no government or official seal imagery, no watermarks.

## Pemeriksaan

Komposisi berupa satu simbol, tanpa teks, menampilkan figur, mahkota, dan monumen sesuai brief. Simbol disimpan sebagai PNG dengan alpha. Hasil AI masih memiliki sedikit variasi warna dan tepi organik; cocok sebagai konsep branding dan pratinjau dashboard. Pengembangan master vektor dapat dilakukan setelah arah logo dipilih.
